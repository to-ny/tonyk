This is an album I want to listen to very loudly in my earbuds while screaming and/or crying. I love the energy it has and the emotions it creates in me. I was very fortunate to see them at Sziget a year ago and it only increased my respect and love for the guy/band. I remember it was quite funny at time when the singer would pump the crowd to mosh and we were all in.. It's just that the songs are not that hard! Anyway, a must album for me that won't leave me anytime soon.
